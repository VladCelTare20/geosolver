## Target human proof — EN (exact)

> *∡ denotes directed angles modulo 180°.*
>
> Let ω be the circle with diameter BC; its centre is O, and M, N lie on ω. Let O₁ and O₂ be the centres of (BMR) and (CNR), which meet again at P.
> Let F be the foot of the perpendicular from B to AR.
>
> **Claim 1.** A, B, F, N are concyclic.
>
> *Proof.* ∡BFA = 90° = ∡BNA, since BF ⟂ AR and BC is a diameter of ω.
>
> **Claim 2.** FB = FN.
>
> *Proof.* In (ABFN) the chords FB and FN subtend the equal angles ∠BAF = ∠FAN, because AR bisects ∠BAC.
>
> **Claim 3.** A, F, R, P are collinear.
>
> *Proof.* AM·AB = AN·AC (power of A with respect to ω). So A has equal powers with respect to (BMR) and (CNR), and lies on their radical axis RP. F lies on AR by definition.
>
> **Claim 4.** △OBF ≅ △ONF; in particular FO bisects ∠BFN.
>
> *Proof.* OB = ON, FB = FN (Claim 2), and OF is common.
>
> **Claim 5.** R, N, F, O are concyclic.
>
> *Proof.* ∡RFN = ∡BFN + 90° = 2∡FBN + 90° = 2∡RAC + 90° = ∡BAC + 90° = ∡MCN
> (BF ⟂ AR; FB = FN; A, B, F, N concyclic; AR bisects ∠BAC; ∠BMC = 90°), and, as drawn, ∠MCN = ½∠MON = ∠RON (central angle in ω; OR bisects ∠MON).
>
> Finally, angle chasing with the inscribed angles of (BMRP) and (RNFO) (Claim 5), △OMR ≅ △ONR (OM = ON and OR bisects ∠MON), Claim 4, FB = FN, BF ⟂ AR and the central angles of ω gives ∡(PB, AR) = ∡(BC, AR). As P lies on AR (Claim 3), PB ∥ BC, so P lies on BC. ∎

# alphageometry-rs/src/human — the deterministic human-style proof writer (docs/HUMAN_PROOFS.md)

Up: [../CODEMAP.md](../CODEMAP.md)

Presentation only: reads an `EngineTrace` (engine/export.rs) and the goal's fact closure, never changes what is proved. Entry: `mod.rs:write` (or `for_problem`), `render_en`, `view::engine_json`, `verify`, `repair`.

## Files
- `mod.rs` — `write` runs the pipeline twice, without then with the theorem library (`Ctx.library`), and keeps the lower `score` (HumanCost + 20·similar-triangle steps + 2 per reason beyond 4 in one link + 12·multi-fact links + 8·doubled directed terms (`doubled_directed`) + 4·directed chains + 20·pooled sentences + 6·repeated citations of one fact in a step (`repeated_citations`)); pipeline `Ctx → Writer::first_pass → search::optimise (library) → select → recertify_with_claims → select → Presenter::run → drop_self_reasons → setup_lines → check::check → barem::polish → fill_metrics`; `Opts{deadline (default 2 s), strict}`; HumanCost (`fill_metrics`), `shape_counts` (similar-triangle and theorem steps); `named_circles` and the setup lines (notation, circles, aux, helpers).
- `trace.rs` — `EngineTrace`: facts, the four tables' rows per fact, figure values, pair-variable maps, primes, sine variables (canonicalised by `(v,{p,q},shift)` key).
- `cert.rs` — exact span machinery: `Basis` (fully reduced, tracks combinations), `certify_greedy` (cost-level insertion, then support minimisation).
- `ctx.rs` — fact classes (Hyp, HypReg, TheoremReg, MergeReg, Silent, SilentHyp, Derived, Outside), quotient by hypothesis lines (`Quot`), line/circle objects, `fact_sources` for silent facts.
- `atoms.rs` — admissible human atoms (Inscribed, Thales, TangentChord, PerpBisector, Parallel, Radii, Isosceles, CentralAngle, PowerOfPoint, Midline, Orthocentre) + engine rows (Glue/Line/Engine), each with cost and availability horizon; `build_all` adds the library's theorem atoms when `Ctx.library`. `Atom.via` lists the atoms that justify this atom's hypotheses (rendered as extra reasons of the same link).
- `theorems.rs` — the theorem library: `spec(key, args)` = hypotheses (`Hyp`: proven line/circle/equation, or a configuration read from the figure) and conclusion rows, shared by the writer and the checker; `config_holds` (figure checks); `candidates` (figure-level enumeration over proven lines/circles); `stmt` (displayed statement).
- `library.rs` — theorem atom generation: `Directory` (established statements: closure fact rows and cheap atoms, keyed up to line classes and scaling), `HypEval` (a hypothesis is one established statement or follows from ≤ `SHORT_SUPPORT` = 3 of them), `Builder::theorems` / `refine` / `rejustify` / `propagate_via`.
- `search.rs` — minimum-cost derivation: Knuth's superior-function DP over the acyclic fact hypergraph (`optimise`), node presentation costs (`present_cost`), hyperedge costs (`edge_cost`), `protect_pruned` for the claim re-certification.
- `classify.rs` — fact kinds, statements, obligations (`Obl` = targets with alternative forms + required objects); `centre_obligations`, `central_obligations`, `sq_stmt`, `formula_stmt` (short trig statements), `dehelper`.
- `claims.rs` — `Writer` (certificates, claim selection, `cover`, `goal_obls`) and `Presenter` (blocks, chains, lemma splits, pooled sentences, trig computations and the trig ladder).
- `barem.rs` — the barem presentation pass (docs/HUMAN_PROOFS.md §13.9): undirected chains, one fact per link, promoted theorem steps, no-op link merging, cuts of chains over 8 links, lemma and inline results as their own numbered steps; then `finish` (step numbers, relation tags, plan, goal wording, angle-convention lines).
- `route.rs` — route search over atoms (bidirectional Dijkstra, one atom per link), as-drawn and directed graphs; budgets `BUDGET` 300 / `UNDIRECTED_BUDGET` 200 expansions per search, `TOTAL_BUDGET` 3 500 per writer of which `GOAL_RESERVE` 1 500 is kept for the goal; shapes cached per graph.
- `chain.rs` — shortest single-angle chain search (Dijkstra, budget 20 000), A* ordering of a certificate over composite nodes (`order_items_mode`), `decomps`/`decomps_upto`, `display_node`, ratio splits.
- `expr.rs` — `Expr` evaluation to table rows; undirected interior angles read from the figure.
- `check.rs` — the independent checker (I1 statements, I2 links, I3 templates, I5 order) and the repair loop; `theorem_ok` re-checks a library atom (every hypothesis engine-verified or read from the figure, every conclusion row in the span of the engine's full fact store).
- `aux.rs` — wording for aux constructions: `parse` maps a construction to `AuxWording{key, args}` (keys match ag-studio i18n: `aux.midpoint`, `aux.foot`, …), `en` renders it as "Let X be …".
- `model.rs` — the serialisable data model (§7.2 + the changes listed in docs/HUMAN_PROOFS.md "Data model changes").
- `text.rs` — EN rendering (CLI and tests only; ag-studio renders from keys); thread-local notation, circle names, step numbers and main triangle (`with_proof`).
- `view.rs` — JSON for ag-studio: statements as `{kind,args,points}`, reasons with closure step numbers.

## Notes
- `claims.rs:Writer::certify` — obligations offer every algebraic form of a concyclic/collinear/similar fact; any one in the admissible span suffices. Concyclicity and collinearity are not linear consequences of their premises (only one inscribed form is), so a single fixed form would fail.
- `claims.rs:Writer::cover` — a merged circle/line (≥5 / ≥4 points) is re-proved as a growing chain of 4-/3-point subsets each in the admissible span. `goal_obls` adds the goal fact's own obligations (label `merged`) when the goal is a subset of a merged fact: the last chain states the fact, then "Hence <goal>".
- `classify.rs:centre_obligations` — a concyclic fact may be re-proved as equal distances from a figure point (IMO 2008 P1's six-point circle).
- `classify.rs:sq_stmt` — a theorem with one squared-length row is stated as that equation (`Expr::Sq`); with none it stays `Stmt::Formula` and is never a claim.
- `claims.rs:select` — claim cap `MAX_CLAIMS` = 7 is strict: the extra claims with the fewest users, then the lowest cost, become steps.
- `check.rs:check` — a failing block becomes Raw (its engine steps); claims it demoted are re-cited as `Reason::Engine` and the rest renumbered (`uncite`). A failing conclusion makes the whole proof unavailable: never wrong, at worst absent. `strict` panics instead (tests).
- `check.rs:sentence_ok` — a chain's `then` for a ≥4-point circle or ≥3-point line is accepted when the chain gives any one subset form; the block statement itself is proved separately from engine rows (`stmt_ok`).
- `mod.rs:write` — runs under `catch_unwind` + `quiet_panic`; any panic yields `available: false`. `HP_DEBUG=1` disables the catch and dumps nodes/violations to stderr; `HP_BFS=1` prints chain searches.
- `claims.rs:Presenter::chain_sentences` — stages, first that succeeds wins: trig lemmas (ratio goals with sine atoms) → BFS chain (≤ 8 links) → A* ordering of the certificate (whole, or split at non-integral λ) → lemma split (`lemma_plan`: connected subsets whose sum is a 2-angle or 2-ratio equality, each shown as its own chain, then the main chain cites them as `Reason::Lemma`) → undirected ("as drawn") ordering and halves lemma split → partial plans → pooled. A support with λ denominators other than 1 and 2 is first re-certified with those atoms banned (`Writer::certify_banned`).
- `claims.rs:Presenter::pooled_steps` — a pooled remainder citing more than `MAX_POOLED` = 5 reasons is cut into exact intermediate equalities (≤ 2 terms a side, sums of two named angles allowed; ratios as products with positive exponents); below 5 only clean single-angle intermediates are introduced. After the cut the remainder is tried as a chain again.
- `claims.rs:Presenter::radii_groups` — with `collapse`, hypothesis congruences and isosceles/radii atoms that share a centre become one multi-point atom `[O, P…]`. The rows are all pairs, so each combination term is remapped to its pair row with a sign; an atom whose row does not match ± that pair row is left unmerged.
- `claims.rs:Presenter::trig_lemmas` — for a length goal whose certificate uses sine atoms, find `XY = k·R·trig(v)` for both sides with one sine variable and show each as a `Computation`; identity rows (|sin x| = |sin x|) are dropped.
- `claims.rs:drop_self_reasons` — a fact reason never lists itself (or a hypothesis with the same statement) among its own reasons; `text.rs` also flattens restated reasons in `Because` sentences.
- `claims.rs:Presenter::run` — a claim whose only user restates it through a radii/isosceles atom is replaced by the user's statement (IMO 2023 P2: `XA = XP`, not `△OAX ≅ △OPX`), guarded by a proof check of the new statement.
- `mod.rs:named_circles` — circles with ≥ 2 references get a name (Ω for the main triangle's circumcircle, ω_k for centre O_k, then ω, γ, Γ, σ, τ, κ); a circle line is emitted after the aux point that completes its third defining point, listing only points already defined.
- Debug env vars (stderr only): `HP_TIME` (stage and per-node timings), `HP_POOL` (pooled fallbacks and `pooled_steps`), `HP_ITEMS`, `HP_LEMMA` (lemma candidates).
- `mod.rs:Opts::default` — 2 s deadline; past it the writer stops certifying, emits raw blocks and sets `metrics.timed_out`.
- Determinism: only `BTreeMap`/sorted iteration and integer costs; `tests/human_proofs.rs:output_is_deterministic` compares JSON across runs and threads.
- Tests: `tests/human_proofs.rs` (goldens in `tests/golden/human/*.en.txt`, `HP_BLESS=1` to accept; mutations; fallback; deadline; JGEX corpus with zero checker violations); `tests/human_theorems.rs` (every library theorem: a positive figure plus false instances that neither the writer nor the checker may accept; configuration checks; a corrupted theorem instance is rejected; determinism; deadline).
- `theorems.rs:spec` — a library atom's conclusion rows must be in the span of ALL engine fact rows (`Ctx::store_verified`, an echelon basis built lazily per table), not only the goal's closure: the theorem only changes how a verified fact is justified. Its hypotheses must be in the closure (shown) or engine-verified; configuration hypotheses (`Between`, `Outside`, `SplitAlike`, `OutsideParity`) are read from the figure and the theorem wording says "as drawn".
- `library.rs:Directory::uses` — a hypothesis may not be justified by an atom whose own hypotheses (transitively through `via`) include the theorem's conclusion, so "AB ∥ MN by the converse of the intercept theorem because CA/CN = CB/CM by the intercept theorem with AB ∥ MN" cannot appear.
- `library.rs:Builder::theorems` — runs twice (after the radii atoms, and after the composites so midline/perpendicular-bisector atoms can establish hypotheses); law-of-sines candidates only for sine variables used by the closure; at most `PER_KEY` = 120 atoms per key; congruence atoms (SSS/SAS/ASA/RHS) only for triangle pairs the engine proved congruent.
- `search.rs:optimise` — facts depend only on earlier facts, so Knuth's generalised Dijkstra reduces to one pass in fact order: per node, the current certificate and a re-certification whose atom weights include the total cost of the nodes each atom's sources force (`Writer.extra`); the cheaper total wins. Exact over the generated hyperedges, not over all certificates.
- Debug env vars (stderr only): `HP_LIBSTAT` (both pipelines' availability/score), `HP_LIBRARY=0|only` (one pipeline), `HP_THM` (theorem atoms as generated).
- `barem.rs:polish` — runs only on a checker-clean proof; every transform is re-validated by `check::violations_with` (a batch first, then edit by edit) and the whole pass is reverted if the result has any violation. `HP_BAREM=1` prints rejected edits.
- `barem.rs:split_links` — a multi-fact link is split into one fact per link by trying fact orders (all permutations up to 6 facts, a greedy order up to 14) and keeping the order whose intermediate expressions are displayable and least ugly; collinearities and radii are free and ride with a neighbour. Undirected intermediates have at most two angle terms.
- `barem.rs:undirected` — a directed chain is rewritten with figure angles (∠) only when every link still checks with exact constants (`check::residual_ok`, undirected) and every term scores well (`term_score`: constants 0/90/180 only, coefficients 1, 2 or ½).
- `barem.rs:promote_atoms` — perpendicular bisector, midline, orthocentre, centroid, median-to-hypotenuse, equal tangents and perpendicular-bisector locus atoms always become their own step; Thales, power of a point and parallels when cited twice.
- `barem.rs:finish` — `step` = position, `tag` = cited by a later step, `plan` = up to 3 conclusion premises (not promoted atoms, not angle-sum identities), `goal` = `goal_words`; `check.rs:layout_violations` (I6) re-derives all of these.
- `claims.rs:Presenter::trig_ladder` — a trig lemma `XY = q·R·trig v` is reached through intermediate lemmas of the same shape (`trig_candidates`), each certified with law-of-sines atoms costed +60 so that established lemmas are preferred (`certify_ext`); a lemma is accepted when it lowers the number of law-of-sines triangles the target needs (one-step lookahead in `trig_pair`). Radius segments are merged into R before ordering (`radius_vars`); each link's radius residual is certified separately and cited as radii.
- `claims.rs:route_now` / `route_drawn` — re-routing in the presenter: a pooled part or a chain that does not give its conclusion is re-routed directed (3 000 angle / 1 200 ratio expansions); a chain with a non-unit directed multiple (2∡) or a still-pooled angle part is re-routed as drawn (2 500 per form; collinear targets also get reference-ray forms ∠XVZ = ∠YVZ from `classify.rs:coll_targets`), and a pooled angle part first tries the as-drawn ordering of its own certificate. `halved_lemma` re-routes a doubled directed lemma for the halved statement.
- `theorems.rs` identity atoms `SineSupplement` / `SineComplement` (`is_identity`): conclusion rows are trig identities, not store rows; hypothesis `Hyp::EqOrHalf` (the angle relation, or twice it plus the figure); generated only for length goals (`Figure.trig_goal`) with a sine the closure uses. `library.rs:hyp_justification` picks what the hypothesis cites, smallest tier first (matching central angle + its base isosceles, then no engine rows, then all); `rejustify_identities` repeats it once every atom exists.
- `atoms.rs` `CentralAngle` with a fifth argument equal to the fourth is the reflex form "inscribed angle ∠AZB = 180° − ½∠AOB"; `check.rs:reflex_inscribed` re-reads that configuration from the figure.
- `chain.rs:display_node` — no directed expression with a fractional coefficient (½∡ is only defined mod 90°) and no figure-angle expression with a 0°/180° angle; `barem.rs:display` applies the same rule.
- `barem.rs` later passes: `dedupe_restated` (a radii/isosceles reason restated by another reason), `absorb_by_triangles` (two links that one similar/congruent triangle pair gives alone become one link), `restated_line_fact` (∡(l, m) = 0° from "l ∥ m" becomes a plain sentence), `redisplay_drawn` (an as-drawn chain is re-displayed, or flipped term by term to 180° − x, when its `term_score` drops). `term_score` charges a difference of angles 2 and a lone negative angle 4.
- `mod.rs:named_circles` — for a goal OX = OK with O a circle centre, K is not listed on the circle (that is what is being proved).
- More debug env vars: `HP_ROUTE` (route splits and paths, re-routes), `HP_ROUTE_EDGES`, `HP_COORDS` (point coordinates, for reviewers), `HP_SINES`, `HP_TRIG`, `HP_BFS`.
- `check.rs:stmt_ok` — a block statement may be established by its own body (a chain or a non-empty `Because`), which is what lets barem move a result into its own step.
- `barem.rs:median_hypotenuse` (first pass of `polish`) — a length block `OX = OY` whose body is a doubled directed chain (∡XOY = 2∡…) becomes "O is the midpoint of the hypotenuse PQ of right △PXQ, so …" when the figure has O the midpoint of PQ and a right angle at X; the atom's hypotheses (right angle, OP = OQ, P, O, Q collinear) are re-checked by the checker, never taken from the figure. `promote_atoms` skips an atom that is already the sole reason of a step stating its conclusion.
- `barem.rs:vertical_angles` — a collinearity proved by one equal-angle link plus a hypothesis line becomes "∠PVX = ∠QVY; as P, V, Q are collinear, these are vertical angles" (undirected terms, residual-checked).
- `barem.rs:merge_same_reasons` — consecutive links with identical reasons become one link; `drop_setup_radii` keeps radii in angle chains (they carry the isosceles step).
- `claims.rs:line_lemma` — a doubled directed angle chain (2∡IAP = 2∡APX) is replaced by a collinearity lemma among the statement's points (IMO 2023 P2: A, I, X collinear) when the lemma's row is in the admissible span and routes with λ = ±1 in a graph that contains it; `check.rs:establishes` accepts a chain whose `then` is that lemma.
- `claims.rs:human_justification` — an engine/glue row with no block behind it is cited as the human atom with the same quotient row (Thales, isosceles, …) instead of a bare engine fact.
- `atoms.rs:prefer_isosceles` — a central-angle atom costs one more than an isosceles/radii atom with the same quotient row, so "OB = OC" is preferred to "∠BOC = 2∠A" when both give the row. `Atom.pts` caches the atom's points (filled at the end of `build_all`).
- `library.rs:hyp_justification` — the exact row is tried in every pass before the doubled row (`justify_with(.., doubled)`).
- `cert.rs:Basis` — echelon form keyed by the largest pivotable variable, rows stored with their combinations; same span and the same inserted rows as the fully reduced form it replaced.
- `chain.rs:order_items_mode` — memoised per (node, shape) per mask; a pair expansion that shares no variable with the current node is skipped for angle tables unless half-angles are in play with fewer than three classes.
- `text.rs` (and both ag-studio renderers): a similarity/congruence step names its criterion from its body (`criterion`: AA/SAS/SSS/ASA, RO UU/LUL/LLL/ULU); a cyclic hypothesis in an angle chain reads as inscribed angles (`inscribed_link`); the radical-axis theorem reads "Z has the same power with respect to …" (`radical_text`); a perpendicular-bisector step cites "by (k)" on the equality that step gave.
- `HP_TIME2=1` — presenter stage timers with expansion budgets (`claims.rs:Reporter`), stderr only.
- Displayed figures (`geo.rs:textbook_triangle`, seeds ≤ `TEXTBOOK_SEEDS`) are acute scalene triangles with angles 35°–82° and pairwise gaps ≥ 6°, so as-drawn angle expressions match a textbook figure; verification and `build_instances` are unaffected.

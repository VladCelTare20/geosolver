# alphageometry-rs/src/theory — theorem identities, what each is derived from, and the ban that keeps a proof from citing the theorem it proves

Up: [../CODEMAP.md](../CODEMAP.md)

## Files
- `mod.rs` — `Thm` (one identity per citable theorem, 66), `INFO` (key, EN/RO name, `Kind`, `avoidable`, `uses` edges each with a one-line reason, `cited_by`), `Forbid` (u128 bitset; `closure_of` = the theorems plus everything that transitively uses them), the thread-local `current()`/`with_forbid` for the metric provers, and the maps from every citation the system makes to identities: `of_atom` (human `AtomKey`), `of_theorem_key` (`TheoremKey`), `ENGINE_NAMES`/`of_name` (engine rule strings), `of_reason`, `of_fact` (uses `Fact.thm`, the tag a gated rule sets). `graph_text` is `ddar --theory`.
- `detect.rs` — `guard(problem)`: which library theorems the goal *states*, on a hypothesis-only tracked closure, within `DETECT_BUDGET` (400 ms). `GEO_NO_THEORY_GUARD=1` disables it.
- `audit.rs` — independent fail-closed checks: `raw`/`raw_log` (engine facts), `human` (blocks: engine facts, atoms, theorem keys, raw steps, converse inscribed-angle use), `text` (theorem names in rendered text, `MARKERS`).
- `tests.rs` — table order, acyclicity, forbidden sets, every `AtomKey`/`TheoremKey`/engine rule string/`Reason` variant mapped (a new rule with no identity fails here).

## Notes
- `mod.rs:Kind` — `Calculus` (angle chasing, segment arithmetic, circle definition, trig identities) is never detected or banned. `InscribedAngle` is an `Axiom` built into the closure (`avoidable: false`): a statement of it is reported Not proven (circular) at once. The similarity criteria and the law of sines are bannable axioms. Congruence criteria are theorems derived from the matching similarity with ratio 1, but neither criteria nor congruences are *detected* as statements (`detectable`): JGEX exercises (gao_L022-1, E051-31, E051-6) state them in passing.
- `mod.rs:Thm::LawOfCosines` — uses Pythagoras: under a Pythagoras ban the ratio prover's T7 cosine rows (`ratio/trig/cos.rs`) are off, since cos 90° = 0 makes them Pythagoras.
- `detect.rs:goal_theorems` — a goal is T's statement when T's instance rows (with its hypothesis rows and the given line incidences) alone give the goal, T's angle hypotheses each follow from one given fact plus incidences (`Given::direct`), and the goal does not already follow from the flat given span (`any(None, true)`). Cyclic goals: a restatement when all points are on a given circle; `InscribedConverse` when one given fact implies an inscribed form. `distmeq` goals with constant 1 become a Ratio row (`distmeq_alts`); other constants are numeric exercises, not statements. Library candidates come from `human::theorems::candidates` over given lines and circles (including equal-distance groups); `local_specs` adds Midline, Isosceles, Thales, ThalesConverse, CentralAngle, PerpBisector, Orthocentre, EulerLine, Incentre, Menelaus, Ceva, Centroid, Circumcentre, NinePointCircle, PowerOfPoint, RadicalAxis, TangentChord. `triangle_specs` (criteria) only with `GEO_DETECT_CRITERIA`.
- Measured: IMO 30 → 0 detected (slowest 0.023 s); JGEX 231 → 6 (reim ×2, orthocentre ×2, miquel, nine_point_circle), slowest 0.042 s (`ddar --theory-corpus`).
- `synthetic.rs:stated_theorems`, `ratio.rs:stated_theorems` — the metric counterpart: a symbolic goal (no numeric constant) that follows from one theorem step plus symbolic given steps. Numeric exercises ("AM² = 27") use the theorem; they are not its statement.
- `audit.rs:text` — substring markers with `minus` exclusions ("converse of thales" does not count as Thales). It is the last line of defence; structural audits run first.
- Enforcement points: engine rules gated by `Ddar::forbid` (`engine.rs`, `engine/classics.rs`, `engine/sqlen.rs`, `engine/trig.rs`; similarity by criterion, concyclic forcing as `InscribedConverse`/`ThalesConverse`); `runner.rs:audited` drops any proof whose steps map to a banned identity; human `atoms.rs` skips banned atoms, `check.rs:forbidden_violations` (rule I8) and `human::write`'s final audit make the writer fail closed; the metric provers read `current()`.

# alphageometry-rs/src — the DDAR engine (Rust port of AlphaGeometry2's symbolic core)

Up: [../../CODEMAP.md](../../CODEMAP.md)

## Files
- `lib.rs` — the crate root (library name `ddar`): the public modules and the re-exports `Ddar`, `Predicate`, `Problem`.
- `geo.rs` — the `.geo` language: parse, expand constructions, sample a figure, compile to predicates. Point cap counts expanded points.
- `predicate.rs` — the low-level AlphaGeometry predicate language and parser. `Problem::parse` accepts any relation name, arity and f64 (the engine's own callers and tests rely on that); input from outside goes through `lowlevel.rs`.
- `lowlevel.rs` — `parse_checked`: what a hand-written low-level problem must satisfy before `Ddar` sees it — at most `MAX_POINTS` (100, shared with `geo.rs` and the corpus translator), finite coordinates (|x| ≤ 1e6), only the relations `Ddar` implements with their exact point/constant counts, and every hypothesis true at the given coordinates. `goal_holds` reads the goal off the coordinates; `conjuncts` lowers a `midp`/`simtri`/`contri` goal as the corpus translator does (`corpus.rs:lower_engine_goal`).
- `engine.rs` — DDAR deductive closure; goals include `acompute` (angle) and `rcompute` (ratio fixed to a constant).
- `elim_core.rs`, `elimination.rs`, `lincomb.rs`, `rational.rs` — exact Gaussian elimination over rationals (angles, ratios, distances, squared lengths). With tracking on, `ElimCore.fact_rows` keeps each fact's added row (read only by `engine/export.rs`).
- `fingerprint.rs` — linear fingerprints of `LinComb`s mod 2^61−1; can only rule an equality out.
- `aux_search.rs` — LM-free auxiliary-point search over DDAR: the classical construction library, `WarmBase`, and the entry points (`solve_max_until` → `aux_rollout::search`; `depth1_solvers` and `rollouts_until` split it for ag-studio's best mode).
- `aux_virtual.rs` — new candidate kinds: virtual lines (parallel / perpendicular / tangent / isogonal through a point) met with figure lines and circles, intersections with circles the closure proved, harmonic conjugates.
- `aux_score.rs` — coincidence score: extra figure lines, circles and equal-distance classes through a candidate beyond its defining loci. Ranking only.
- `aux_rollout.rs` — scored pool, depth-1 sweep, randomised multi-point rollouts, minimisation.
- `metric.rs` — metric goals: `solve` is `Ok` only for a Euclidean proof; otherwise `MetricError::{Refuted, NoProof{evidence}, Failed}`. `check_numerically` is evidence, never a proof.
- `synthetic.rs`, `ratio.rs` — classical theorem-citing proofs for length / ratio goals.
- `budget.rs` — a thread-local deadline for code with no deadline parameter: `within(deadline, f)` runs `f`, and `check()` (in the outer point loops of `synthetic.rs`/`ratio.rs` and every `Ddar::base_closure` pass) unwinds out of it once the deadline has passed. A no-op outside `within`.
- `certify.rs` — `Certifier`: derives a proposed fact with the DDAR closure and returns the numbered derivation.
- `proof.rs` — provenance and readable proof text. `Reason::Formula(rule, text, points)` is a step whose statement is spelled out (`{i}` = the i-th point's name, filled at render time so renamed aux points stay right).
- `numerics.rs` — f64 geometry oracle.
- `svg.rs` — figure rendering; all text is XML-escaped here.
- `quiet_panic.rs` — one process-wide hook, thread-local mute.
- `runner.rs` — helpers shared by `bin/ddar.rs`, ag-studio and tests; `solve_conjunction_with_proof` proves several goal predicates in one closure and renders one proof of their joint fact closure; `solve_problem_with_trace` returns the proof text, the `EngineTrace` and the goal's fact deps for the human writer.
- `corpus.rs` — reader for the original AlphaGeometry corpus language (`corpus/*.txt`, constructions from `corpus/defs.txt`) → `Problem`.
- `bench.rs` — solve-rate benchmark: `solve_one` (DDAR, then aux search, under a deadline) and `run_corpus` (one child process per problem, killed at budget + grace; `RunConfig.child_flag` picks the child mode; `RunConfig.human_stats` makes each child print one `HUMAN\t` metrics line per proved conjunct, collected into `Outcome.human`).
- `fuzz.rs` — soundness fuzzer: genuinely false variants of corpus problems, each run through DDAR + aux search in its own process; any proof is a soundness bug (exit 2). Run under the bench lock: `flock ~/Projects/geosolver-bench/.bench.lock ddar --fuzz-false corpus/jgex_ag_231.txt --per 8 --budget 10 --jobs 6 --threads 2 --out f.tsv` (cases in `f.tsv.cases.txt`, proofs of any proved case in `f.tsv.proofs/`).

## Notes
- `lowlevel.rs:hypothesis_holds` — `coll` and `cyclic` use the engine's own absolute `ATOM` tests (`force_collinear`/`force_concyclic` panic past them, and three distinct collinear "concyclic" points make `force_concyclic` panic too), the rest the translator's tolerances (`corpus.rs:holds`); `angeq` with a non-integer coefficient, `acompute` and `rcompute` are not checked. Before this, unknown relations, wrong arity, NaN/inf and hypotheses the coordinates contradict all reached the engine and panicked (index out of bounds, `unexpected predicate`, `points not numerically collinear`), and 900 points allocated 8·n³ bytes. Every low-level form the app prints (all 261 corpus translations, `problems.tsv`) passes.
- `geo.rs:compile` — rejects metric goals with `CompileError::MetricGoal`; before this, they compiled to `cong a b a b` and were "proved" trivially.
- `geo.rs:classify_metric_eq` — `angle(a,b,c) = angle(d,e,f)` and `angle(a,b,c) = k` are undirected (`Rel::AngSumEq`), as goals, `point:` constraints and `assume`s: the residual is the undirected angle, and `angsumeq_to_predicate` reads each angle's orientation off the figure, emitting an orientation-matched `eqangle`/`aconst` (`simplify_angeq`) or a general `angeq`. Lowering them straight to `eqangle`/`aconst` (mod 180°, no orientation) proved supplementary angles equal and 120° as 60° (`tests/soundness.rs: undirected_angle_equalities_are_not_directed_ones`, `an_angle_value_is_…`, `an_undirected_angle_hypothesis_…`). A single angle equal to 0°/180° (or outside) is a fatal error pointing to `coll`.
- `geo.rs:goal_fails_in_another_figure` — after `compile_stmts` settles on a goal-true figure it probes up to 8 textbook seeds and 40 general ones (seeds 201..400: right and obtuse triangles, free points on either side of a segment or arc; 2 s cap, interleaved so the cap never drops the general ones); a goal false in any valid figure (or any goal-false build during the search) sets `Compiled::configuration_dependent`, which the product shows as "proved for the configuration drawn only" (`a_goal_that_fails_in_another_valid_figure_is_flagged_as_drawn`, `betweenness_arc_side_and_obtuse_goals_are_flagged_as_drawn`). The test is `Scene::goal_source_holds` (the statement as written: undirected angle sums in degrees, no reduction mod 180°) or `numeric_holds` of the lowered predicate. The lowered `eqangle`/`aconst`/`angeq` re-reads each angle's orientation in every figure, so on its own it is true wherever the directed statement is, and `angle(A,B,D) = angle(A,B,C)` with D on the far side of B, `angle(A,B,C) = angle(A,D,C)` with D on the other arc, or an orthocenter statement in an obtuse triangle all passed it.
- `geo.rs:Scene::goal_source_holds` — `Lowered::source_holds`: for an `AngSumEq` goal the sum of the undirected angles in degrees (`atan2`, tolerance `ANGLE_SUM_TOLERANCE_DEG`) against the constant, never reduced mod 180°. `compile_stmts` turns `Some(false)` into `Compiled::goal_numerically_holds = Some(false)`, so `triangle angle sum = 360` (or 0, 540, a parallelogram's opposite-angle difference = 180, a quadrilateral's sum = 720) is refuted, where the mod-180 `angeq` made every K off by a multiple of 180° "true" (`tests/soundness.rs: an_angle_sum_off_by_a_multiple_of_180_is_numerically_false`, `ag-studio engine.rs: angle_sums_off_by_a_multiple_of_180_are_refuted_on_every_path`). `ddar --geo` prints "Not proven" and exits 1 for a goal the figure contradicts instead of proving it anyway.
- `geo.rs:check_point_names` — declared point names are at most 16 characters (`a_point_name_over_sixteen_characters_is_refused_with_a_precise_error`); a 5,000-character name used to panic the figure renderer.
- `svg.rs:undirected_reading_holds` — an `eqangle`/`aconst` in vertex form (`∠XYZ`) is typeset with `∡` when its undirected reading is false in the figure (`legend_lines`, and `ag-studio` facts); `tests/corpus.rs: every_displayed_undirected_angle_goal_is_true_in_its_figure`.
- Expression parsers cap exponents at 32 and binary operators at 256 per relation (stack overflow and blowup guards).
- `corpus.rs` — premises are exactly the `defs.txt` predicates of each construction; numerics follow its sketch line. Every premise is checked numerically on the sample (a mis-ported sketch is rejected, never fed to DDAR); the figure is resampled until the goal holds, like AG1's `build_problem`. Argument lists missing the new points get them prepended (AG1 `add_clause`). Shapes built from nothing are mirrored with p=1/2 (AG1 `random_rfss`) — the `contri` square/15° problem needs the other orientation. `s_angle a b x y` lowers to `aconst b x b a y`. `simtri`/`contri`/`midp` goals become conjunctions of directed-angle/`cong`/`coll` facts (sufficient for the goal). A `simtri`/`contri` whose triangle is collinear in the figure is refused (`triangle_orientation` → "needs two genuine triangles", also in `lowlevel.rs:check`): both angle conjuncts are 0 = 0 there, which "proved" △ABC ≅ △DEF with BC = 1, EF = 4. The numeric goal check also tests the whole statement (`holds("simtri"/"contri")`: all three side ratios, or sides), not only the conjuncts.
- `bench.rs:solve_one` — a result is `proved` only with an engine proof for every goal conjunct; a numerically false goal that DDAR proves is reported `UNSOUND` (the CLI exits 2).
- `aux_search.rs:hypothesis_circle_members` — antipode-on-circle and pole-of-chord candidates may only use points on the circle *by hypothesis cong chains*. Numeric membership let IMO 2008 P6 assert its own goal (`tests/soundness.rs: aux_search_does_not_assume_numeric_circle_membership`).
- `aux_search.rs:solve_max_until` — runs `aux_rollout::search` (`AUX_LEGACY=1` restores the old full depth-1 sweep + rank-first deepening). It is also ag-studio's default solve (`engine.rs:deductive_flow`). `depth1_solvers` sweeps the same pool but keeps *every* solving construction (named as `search` names its result) for the shortest-proof hunt, and `rollouts_until` is `search` minus the sweep for a caller that already ran it. `solve_max`/`solve_with_aux` (tests, `--max`) still use the old search. `WarmBase::check_until` is `check` with a deadline.
- `aux_rollout.rs:search` — pool = classical library + new kinds with ≥1 extra incidence, ranked by `2·inc + heuristic`. Exhaustive depth-1 sweep, then rounds: `ROUND_WIDTH` = 12 first points (fixed, not per thread: at 4·threads, 1/3/8/15 threads found four different constructions for IMO 2019 P2, and the app's 15 threads spent 5× longer per round than the 3-thread benchmark, so IMO 2021 P3 flipped between proved and timeout at 60 s; with 12 it is proved at 23 s and every thread count explores the same sets — `tests/robustness.rs: the_rollout_search_finds_the_same_construction_at_any_thread_count`) drawn by weight `exp(0.9·inc + 0.25·heur)`, each extended 48 times by 1–3 more points (from the pool, or from the lazily built child pool of one of the top `PARENTS` = 512 points already drawn: its top `CHILD_CAP` = 192 candidates built on it — the caps bound memory, unbounded child pools reached 1.8 GB on `morley`). Each first point is closed once (`WarmBase::extend`) and its continuations checked warm. Seeds come from a hash of the problem + round + index and `find_map_first` returns the lowest index, so the found set does not depend on thread timing or count, only on how many rounds fit before the deadline and on when the wall-clock lemma phase cuts in (kept on the clock on purpose: a round-count trigger would never reach the lemma phase on heavy problems whose rounds take seconds). Tried sets are deduplicated; a found set is minimised by dropping points (and their children) while the goal still follows.
- `aux_rollout.rs` — points with (numerically) equal coordinates are never combined in one rollout: DDAR keeps no pair variables for numerically identical points, so two definitions of one point could be identified without a proof.
- **Double points** (`aux_search.rs:landing`, `aux_rollout.rs:double_point_ok`) — a top-level candidate that lands on an existing point P (within `1e-10·scale`) is kept, snapped onto P, when no predicate takes a direction or length between it and P, all its predicates hold numerically, at least one holds of P in the base closure and at least one does not (an unexplained incidence: the second construction of P a proof identifies with P). Only `engine.rs:merge_points` (two non-tangent objects, or two objects proved tangent) ever identifies the two. Score: fixed `DOUBLE_INC` = 3 (+2 when P is a goal point), not the coincidence score, which counts every object through P. Child pools never hold doubles; the rollout guard above still keeps two of them apart. `AUX_DOUBLES=0` turns them off. IMO 2011 P6 (x = the double point of two tangent circles) is proved through `x1 = circle(pc,pb,a1) ∩ ω` and the tangent merge (`tests/soundness.rs: double_point_candidate_proves_imo_2011_p6`; decoys: `double_points_do_not_assume_numeric_coincidences`, `tangent_merge_needs_a_proved_tangency`).
- `aux_search.rs:coincident_args` — no candidate is built on two numerically identical points (`1e-6·scale`): the arc midpoint of a zero chord satisfies `cong n k n x` anywhere on the circle, and the old search proved a false `coll x o p` with it on a figure with a double point (`tests/soundness.rs: tangent_merge_needs_a_proved_tangency`, first decoy).
- `aux_score.rs:Scorer` — numerically identical points are one point to the equal-distance score (a double point otherwise gave every candidate an incidence).
- `aux_rollout.rs:lemma_phase` — at 30% of the budget (but by `LEMMA_START_CAP`, 18 s, at the latest, so a long budget does not postpone it: idle-box A/B at 120 s, 3 threads, moved the IMO 2021 P3 lemma from 36.3 s to 18.5 s with no change in outcome and left IMO 2008 P6 at 69–70 s), if the rollouts have found nothing: every collinear triple / concyclic quadruple of problem points that holds in the figure but not in the base closure (`open_lemmas`, goal points first, at most 8) becomes the goal of its own short search (at most half the phase each; its sweep covers only the 1000 best-ranked candidates, then rollouts — a full sweep cost 5-7x more on a loaded box and starved the lemma). The phase ends at 75% of the budget or after 20 s. Each proved lemma's constructions are added to the problem, and a search on the augmented problem runs to 85% of the budget or 40 s; then the original rollouts resume where they stopped (their seeds are per round, so a set they would have found is found later, not lost — IMO 2008 P6, a round-4 rollout solve at 20-47 s depending on load, is the case the caps protect). Only constructions are carried — never the lemma as a premise — so the final proof is one DDAR closure over hypotheses + aux definitions. Thébault (jgex t5): `coll j k n`, `cyclic a f h n`, then 1 point; IMO 2021 P3: `cyclic b c e f` (d' on AD and circle BCD), then 2 points (`AUX_LEMMAS=0` turns it off).
- `corpus.rs:tangent_half_chord` — a near-zero discriminant (`< 1e-12·r²`) in a line–circle or circle–circle intersection is a tangency: both points are the foot. Before, rounding split the double point into two points ~1e-6·r apart (IMO 2011 P6's x was 3.3e-8 off the line of centres, 1e4·ATOM).
- `aux_rollout.rs:build_pool` — child pools are built with `parallel = false`: they are initialised inside rayon tasks through `OnceLock`, and a nested parallel iterator there could steal a task that waits on the same lock.
- `aux_virtual.rs` — every candidate asserts exactly two loci (a line or circle each); intersections that are near-tangent or near-parallel are skipped, so no relation among existing points is implied. `tests/soundness.rs: new_aux_kinds_do_not_assume_numeric_coincidences` audits each kind next to a free point that only numerically satisfies the goal.
- `aux_search.rs:circle3`, `flat` — no circle through, or triangle centre of, a numerically flat triple (a triple collinear because the goal says so gave a giant "circumcircle" and asserted `cyclic` on collinear points); candidates farther than 100× the figure span are dropped.
- `aux_search.rs:Construction::tpl` — `desc` with point ids as tokens (`tok`), re-rendered after rollout points are renumbered and renamed (`aux_rollout.rs:finish`).
- `engine.rs:deduction_closure_until` — gives up between passes past a deadline (returns `false`, so a check reads "not proved"); the rollout and sweep checks use it, the old search and the base closure do not, hence still the process kill in `bench.rs`.
- `metric.rs:solve_until` — the provers had no deadline and O(n⁴–n⁶) point loops: Ptolemy plus 89 free points ran past 300 s, and in the app the worker was SIGKILLed at its hard limit with no figure. Now: a goal that fails in the drawn figure is refuted first (no derivation of it could pass `checked_proof`); the provers run under `budget::within`; after a stop, the numeric check decides between `NoProof { reason: TIME_LIMIT_REASON }` (holds in every sample), `TimedOut` (holds in the drawn figure only — Ptolemy depends on the drawn convex order, so "refuted" would mislabel it) and `Refuted`. `tests/soundness.rs: metric_provers_stop_at_their_deadline_and_never_prove_past_it`, `a_metric_goal_false_in_the_drawn_figure_is_refuted_before_the_provers_run`.
- `geo.rs:SampledFigure::without_idle_points` — both metric provers drop points that no hypothesis, length constraint or metric hypothesis mentions and that are not in the goal (89 stray `free` points made Ptolemy run out of time; now 0.6 s). `ratio.rs:gather_generic_similar` visits each pair of triangles once by index; it used to also record every pair in a `BTreeSet` that could never reject one, which on 28 concyclic points grew past the worker's 2 GB cap.
- `synthetic.rs`, `ratio.rs` — coordinates only propose candidates and read configuration (order, orientation, inside/outside); every cited fact is symbolic or DDAR-certified (`certify.rs`): generic similar triangles (`ratio.rs:certified_products`), the intercept parallel, the bisector, Stewart's ratio (`synthetic.rs:derived_ratio`). Circles come from symbolic equal-radius classes (`synthetic.rs:equal_radius_circles`).
- `synthetic.rs:prove_euclidean_guarded`, `ratio.rs:prove_ratio_guarded`, `metric.rs:solve_until_guarded` — a symbolic goal that one theorem step proves from the symbolic givens states that theorem (`stated_theorems`): the pass is rerun under `theory::with_forbid(closure)`, where the gated rules are off and `synthetic.rs` adds `rule_leg` (leg theorem from AA similarity, DDAR-certified foot), `rule_line_squares` (squared lengths of collinear points, `SegmentArithmetic`) and `add_feet` (feet of the goal's right angles when Pythagoras is banned; of goal points onto goal lines when Stewart/Apollonius/parallelogram/British flag are). The guarded metric solve also text-audits the proof (`audited`) and reports the stated theorems to its callers. With nothing detected the passes are the old ones (detection only reads the steps).
- `synthetic.rs:prove_euclidean` — before the theorem rules, DDAR-certified right angles, midpoints and (aux pass) equal lengths are added; the aux pass also introduces intersections of goal segments and the centres of quadrilaterals on the goal points. All certified steps share one printed derivation (`render`).
- `ratio.rs:prove_ratio` — the product engine runs on a fresh figure, so the monomial engine's perpendicular feet never leak into its derivations. Its candidates (similar triangles, collinear splits, the bisector theorem) wait in `pending` until certified; a lone named-theorem citation is rejected as circular.
- `ratio.rs:prove_products` — any homogeneous degree. Degree 2 runs the original gathers first, unchanged; only when that fails (and only with `t2`, i.e. not inside the old `aux_search`) are the T2 cofactor rows `m − m[s1→s2]` added, each congruence a pending `cong` certified by DDAR. Golden proof text: `tests/metric_golden.rs`.
- `ratio.rs:prove_ratio` stage order — S1 monomial, S3 products (+T2), S4 old aux search; only then the trig stages (S2 on the kept S1 figure, then S5/S6). Running S2 before S4 changed two golden proofs, so trig never runs while a trig-free stage could still succeed.
- `ratio.rs:llower` — `sin(angle(a,b,c))` lowers to the atom `Sin(b;a,c)` (the interior angle is in [0, π], so its sine is |sin|) when `sin_atom` accepts the corner; a goal that only restates one law-of-sines row is refused by the lone-headline guard.
- `ratio.rs:certified_products` lone guard — besides one lone headline row, rejects one headline spread over several multiples when everything else is `PStep::support`.
- `engine.rs:transfer_dist_arc_mul` — both maps are keyed by the *simplified* arc / chord (the lookups always were), and the stored raw value is kept so the transfer cites the source's reduction too. Before, the raw insert made the lookup miss almost always (completeness only). Corpus at jobs 4 × threads 2: imo 25/30, jgex 227/231, 0 UNSOUND, both unchanged; 16 proofs change length (bench `trig/p7a-arc-chord/compare.txt`).
- `engine.rs:centre_strictly_left` — the arc-chord transfer's configuration branch (which way round a chord is read) needs the centre clearly on one side of the chord (relative margin 1e-9); a diameter is skipped. On a special figure (fuzz case jgex E051-26~hs2: a rectangle that is generically an isosceles trapezoid) the side of a diameter is rounding noise, and reading it paired the arcs wrongly and proved a false GE = EF. Regression: `tests/soundness.rs:arc_chord_transfer_reads_no_side_off_a_diameter`.
- `elim_core.rs:add_constraint` — pivot = min (rank, usages); every variable has rank 1 unless created by `new_var_ranked` (the DDAR sine variables, rank 0), so without trig the pivot choice is unchanged.
- `engine.rs:search_similitude` — the image-vs-antihomologous pairing is read off the figure only when line ZP meets circle 1 in two numerically distinct points; at a tangency both pairings coincide, so the choice would be a coincidence, not a configuration (found by the fuzzer, four false statements: `tests/soundness.rs: similitude_pairing_is_not_read_off_a_tangent_coincidence`, `..._tangent_line`).
- `engine.rs:search_radical_axis` — the inside/outside branch is read from chord-end order, not from the conclusion. Two circle objects sharing three symbolic points (two of them numerically identical) may be one circle, whose "radical axis" is anything: such a pair is skipped. Found by the fuzzer through double points (`tests/soundness.rs: double_points_and_coinciding_circles_prove_nothing_false`); the DDAR half was a pre-existing hole. Two objects sharing only two points can be one circle too (IMO 2008 P1: circles (a1 a2 b1) and (a1 a2 b2) are one circle by the theorem, so the eqratio is automatic and x is free; the pinned figure put x on a1a2 and the rule proved `coll x a1 a2`, a statement false on generic figures, on wf/final as well): every pair must be numerically distinct circles (`circles_distinct`, centre or radius apart by more than `1e-6·r`, a non-degeneracy check). `tests/soundness.rs: radical_axis_needs_two_distinct_circles` (with a distinct-circles control).
- `engine.rs:has_unmerged_double` / `aux_search.rs:WarmBase::check_all` — a proof is accepted only when every aux point sitting on an earlier point has been merged with it by a proved merge; an unmerged double point means the closure carried a numerically identical pair whose objects may coincide.
- `elimination.rs:force_zero` / `force_one` (all four tables) — a row the figure contradicts is refused and counted (`ElimCore.rejected`, `Ddar::rejected_rows`), in release builds too; debug builds still assert. Before, release builds added it: the angle bisector theorem with its apex on line bc (asserted only `eqangle a b a x a x a c`, which a point of the line satisfies as 0 = 0) forced |xb|/|xc| = |ab|/|ac|, 1/2 = 5/2, and proved that ratio (`tests/soundness.rs: bisector_theorem_needs_a_triangle`). A refusal costs completeness only; a rule that needs a non-degenerate figure checks it itself (`engine.rs:clearly_off_line`, 1e-7 of the longest side, used by `search_bisector_theorem`).
- `engine.rs:force_pred_because`, `force_similar`, `force_concyclic` — rows are built from raw pair quantities, never cached normal forms, so `ElimCore::add_constraint` records the facts its reduction used. `cong o x o a` forced after `cong o t o a` was stored citing itself alone, and proofs omitted the other radius (`tests/soundness.rs: proof_steps_cite_the_rows_that_normalised_them`); a similarity's angle rows now cite their normalising facts directly instead of only through the similarity step.
- `engine.rs:pred_degenerate` — a predicate naming a direction or length of two numerically identical points (or a line/circle through too few distinct points) has no variables: as a goal it is not proved, as a hypothesis it is skipped (`cyclic_with_centers` keeps its two-point branch). It used to panic (`tests/soundness.rs: degenerate_goals_are_unproved_not_panics`). `centre_strictly_left` and `engine/sqlen.rs:sq_from_perpendicular_lines` skip numerically identical pairs too (a point 9e-16 from another panicked the arc-chord transfer: `near_identical_points_do_not_panic_the_closure`).
- `engine/classics.rs:line_meet` — two lines sharing two symbolic points (an unmerged numerically identical pair) may be one line: no meet, so no Menelaus/Ceva on them.
- `engine.rs:proved_tangency` — the tangent counterpart of the non-tangent merge: a numerically identical pair on two circles with distinct centres, one of the pair on the proved line of centres (or on a line proved ⟂ the radius and a circle), is one point (`Reason::TangentMerge`). Premises: the facts putting both on both objects + the line-of-centres / perpendicularity fact.
- `engine.rs:check_pred_deps` — cites the merges that retired a goal point (a goal read through `subst` used to print without them).
- `engine.rs:PairIds` — per-pass keys for the O(n³) searches (similar, concyclic, bisector): a triangle's angle/ratio key is a fingerprint difference of two cached pair values, O(1). A fingerprint match is acted on only after an exact comparison of interned normal forms (`ang`/`rat`, memoised in the thread-local stamped `TripleMemo`); a coefficient with no residue switches the pass to exact ids (`fp_ok`). Shared across the three searches until the next `update_cache`.
- `engine.rs` speed work keeps the force order: bucket maps are only probed, never iterated; concyclic's group map is keyed by `&LinComb`, which hashes like the old `Angle` key. wf/speed kept proofs byte-identical to a764596; the engine/ rules (wf/rules) change proofs on purpose.
- `engine.rs:search_similar` — degenerate triangles are skipped by `force_collinear`'s tolerance (`numerically_flat`) as well as `orientation`: a long base with sub-ATOM height would otherwise force a bogus ratio.
- `engine.rs:row_already_concyclic`, `line_holds` — skip forces that `force_concyclic`/`force_collinear` would exit from as no-ops. They rely on `triple_to_circle`/`pair_line` mapping every numerically distinct triple/pair of active points to the one object holding it; `line_holds` also repeats `force_collinear`'s numeric check so a would-be panic still happens.
- `engine.rs:force_*_with` — premises are computed lazily, after the no-op exit; nothing mutates in between, so the cited facts are unchanged.
- `aux_search.rs:try_solve` — at depth 1 the `WarmBase` closure also answers "does the base already prove the goal" (`proves_goal`), so a branch costs one cold closure, not two.
- `synthetic.rs` rectangle detection needs `AD ∥ BC` or a third right angle; `AB ∥ DC` is implied by two right angles and proves nothing.

- `fuzz.rs:generate` — four case kinds. `goal`: mutated goal (swap/transpose points, re-segment, other predicate over the same points) kept only if false on every one of `--samples` figures where the original goal holds. `hyp-generic`: one construction dropped (or replaced by `free`/`triangle`/`quadrangle`) and the goal false on every figure of the weaker problem. `hyp-special`: that same weaker problem pinned to the ORIGINAL figure, where the dropped fact and the goal still hold numerically — catches a rule that reads an unstated fact off the coordinates. `degenerate`: original problem with a point pinned onto an earlier one or a collinear base triangle; unsound only if a conjunct false on that figure is proved.
- `fuzz.rs` — every case is corpus text with pinned coordinates (`corpus.rs:render_problem`, shortest round-trip floats), so the child reproduces the parent's figure exactly. Generation is deterministic per (`--seed`, problem name).
- `fuzz.rs:generate_geo` — `--fuzz-false <dir>`: metric goals of every `.geo` program under it (goal: swap/transpose points, change a constant, scale a side; hypothesis: drop an `assume` or a `point:` constraint, or replace a definition by `free`), kept only if clearly false (rel. error > 1e-4) on every sampled instance — instance 0 is the provers' own figure. Text is `geo: <stmts; ...> ? <goal>`. `solve_geo_case` calls `synthetic::prove_euclidean` and `ratio::prove_ratio` directly, bypassing `metric::solve`'s figure backstop, so a wrong derivation is seen even though the product would reject it.
- `fuzz.rs:verdict` — a non-degenerate case is false as a theorem, so any proof is `UNSOUND`, even when the goal holds on the pinned figure (`hyp-special`).

## Subfolders
- [engine/](engine/CODEMAP.md) — classical closure rules (squared lengths, Menelaus/Ceva, bisector concurrency, triangle equality) and the trig rows of the DDAR closure (law of sines, multiple-angle products, converse; `--trig`, fallback by default).
- [human/](human/CODEMAP.md) — the deterministic human-style proof writer, its checker and data model.
- `bin/` — `ddar.rs`, the engine CLI (`--help` lists modes); `--human` / `--human-json` print the human proof, `--human-stats <file>` (corpus) writes the writer's metrics per proof. `HP_SAVE=<dir>` (with `--human`) saves every solved conjunct as one line `name, k, first aux point, aux descriptions (U+001F separated), low-level problem`; `--human-replay <dir> --human` re-runs only the writer on those lines (384 proofs in about 9 s), and `HP_LINT=1` adds `LINT name k rule step detail` lines (`human::lints`).
- [ratio/](ratio/CODEMAP.md) — the trigonometric layer of the metric ratio prover.
- [theory/](theory/CODEMAP.md) — theorem identities and their dependency graph, statement detection, the ban (`Forbid`) and the audits that keep a proof from citing the theorem it proves.

Claim 1. T, E, S, O are concyclic.
Proof. ∡STO = ∡SBE + 90° = ∡SNE + 90° = ∡OSE = ∡SEO (OP₁ ⟂ BE; BNSE cyclic; NS is a diameter; central angle ∠NOE = 2∠NSE), so T, E, S, O are concyclic.
Claim 2. T, P, X, O are concyclic.
Proof. Angle chasing with OP₁ ⟂ BE, the inscribed angles in (ABCNSEP), BDLP cyclic, PX is tangent to the circle centred at O₁, DL ∥ BC, ON is the perpendicular bisector of BC and ON = OP gives T, P, X, O are concyclic.
Claim 3. T, P, E, D are concyclic.
Proof. Angle chasing with the inscribed angles in (ABCNSEP), BDLP cyclic, DL ∥ BC, Claim 1, ON is the perpendicular bisector of BC, OA = OE and central angle ∠AOE = 2∠APE gives T, P, E, D are concyclic.
Claim 4. △OAX ≅ △OPX.
Proof. Angle chasing with Claim 2, the inscribed angles in (ABCNSEP), NS is a diameter, PX is tangent to the circle centred at O₁, Claim 1, Claim 3 and OA = OP gives ∡AOX = ∡XOP. OA : OX = OP : OX (OA = OP). Hence △OAX ≅ △OPX.
Angle chasing with AD ⟂ BC, the inscribed angles in (ABCNSEP), AI bisects ∠BAC, DL ∥ BC, BDLP cyclic, NS is a diameter, PX is tangent to the circle centred at O₁ and XA = XP (Claim 4) gives ∡BAX = ∡XAC. ∎

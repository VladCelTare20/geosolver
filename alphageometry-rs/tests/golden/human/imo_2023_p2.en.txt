∡ denotes directed angles modulo 180°.
Let Ω be the circumcircle of triangle ABC, with centre O; N, S, E, P lie on Ω.
Let ω₁ be the circle centred at O₁ through B, D, L, P.

Claim 1. T, E, S, O are concyclic.
Proof. ∡STO = ∡SBE + 90° = ∡SNE + 90° = ∡OSE = ∡SEO (OT ⟂ BE; inscribed angles in Ω; NS is a diameter; central angle ∠NOE = 2∠NSE), so T, E, S, O are concyclic.
Claim 2. T, P, X, O are concyclic.
Proof. ∡TXP = ∡XBP + ∡BDP = 2∡SAP + ∡BPD = ∡BPD + ∡NOP = ∡BLD + ∡NOP = ∡LBC + ∡NOP = ∡(BE, OP) + 90° = ∡TOP (PX is tangent to ω₁; inscribed angles in Ω; ON = OP, inscribed angles in Ω; inscribed angles in ω₁; DL ∥ BC; ON is the perpendicular bisector of BC, NB = NC (inscribed angles in Ω, AI bisects ∠BAC); OT ⟂ BE), so T, P, X, O are concyclic.
Claim 3. T, P, E, D are concyclic.
Proof. ∡ETD = ∡EON = ∡(OE, BC) + 90° = ∡(OE, DL) + 90° = ∡OEB + ∡BPD + 90° = ∡OEA + ∡APD + 90° = ∡EPD (Claim 1; ON is the perpendicular bisector of BC, NB = NC (inscribed angles in Ω, AI bisects ∠BAC); DL ∥ BC; inscribed angles in ω₁; inscribed angles in Ω; OA = OE, central angle ∠AOE = 2∠APE), so T, P, E, D are concyclic.
Claim 4. XA = XP.
Proof. 2∡PSO = 2∡PNS = 2∡PAS = 2∡PBX = 2∡XPD (NS is a diameter; inscribed angles in Ω; inscribed angles in Ω; PX is tangent to ω₁). 2∡APX = 2∡AES + 2∡SPX = 2∡EDP + 2∡OTE = 2∡OTP (inscribed angles in Ω; Claim 1, 2∡PSO = 2∡XPD; Claim 3). ∡AOX = 2∡APO + ∡POX = ∡XOP (OA = OP; Claim 2, 2∡APX = 2∡OTP). Hence △OAX ≅ △OPX (OA = OP), so XA = XP.
2∡BCA = 2∡EAC = 2∡LBC = 2∡BLD = 2∡BPD (AD ⟂ BC; inscribed angles in Ω; DL ∥ BC; inscribed angles in ω₁). 2∡IAP = 2∡SAP = 2∡CAP + 2∡DBC = 2∡APB + 2∡BDP = 2∡APX (NS is a diameter; inscribed angles in Ω; inscribed angles in Ω, 2∡BCA = 2∡BPD; PX is tangent to ω₁). ∡BAX = 2∡IAC + ∡CAX = ∡XAC (AI bisects ∠BAC; XA = XP (Claim 4), 2∡IAP = 2∡APX), so ∡BAX = ∡XAC. ∎

Claim 1. T, E, S, O are concyclic.
Proof. ∡STO = ∡SBE + 90° = ∡SNE + 90° = ∡OSE = ∡SEO (OP₁ ⟂ BE; BNSE cyclic; NS is a diameter; central angle ∠NOE = 2∠NSE), so T, E, S, O are concyclic.
Claim 2. T, P, X, O are concyclic.
Proof. ∡TXP = ∡XBP + ∡BDP = 2∡SAP + ∡BPD = ∡BPD + ∡NOP = ∡BLD + ∡NOP = ∡LBC + ∡NOP = ∡(BE, OP) + 90° = ∡TOP (PX is tangent to the circle centred at O₁; ABSP cyclic; ON = OP, ANSP cyclic; BDLP cyclic; DL ∥ BC; ON is the perpendicular bisector of BC; OP₁ ⟂ BE), so T, P, X, O are concyclic.
Claim 3. T, P, E, D are concyclic.
Proof. ∡ETD = ∡EON = ∡(OE, BC) + 90° = ∡(OE, DL) + 90° = ∡OEB + ∡BPD + 90° = ∡OEA + ∡APD + 90° = ∡EPD (Claim 1; ON is the perpendicular bisector of BC; DL ∥ BC; BDLP cyclic; ABEP cyclic; OA = OE, central angle ∠AOE = 2∠APE), so T, P, E, D are concyclic.
Claim 4. △OAX ≅ △OPX.

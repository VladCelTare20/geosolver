∡ denotes directed angles modulo 180°.
Let M be the midpoint of BC.
Let N be the midpoint of AC.

Claim 1. △AHB ∼ △MON.
Proof. ∡HAB = ∡(AH, MN) = ∡OMN (midline MN ∥ BA; AH ∥ OM, both ⟂ BC). ∡ABH = ∡(MN, BH) = ∡MNO (midline MN ∥ BA; BH ∥ ON, both ⟂ AC). Hence △AHB ∼ △MON.
∡HAG = ∡OMG (AH ∥ OM, both ⟂ BC). As drawn, AH / AG = AH·MN / (AB·MG) = MO / MG (intercept theorem, AB ∥ MN, midline MN ∥ BA; Claim 1). Hence △AHG ∼ △MOG. ∡OGH = ∡(OM, AH) = ∡OMB + 90° = 0° (△AHG ∼ △MOG; AH ⟂ BC; OM is the perpendicular bisector of BC), so O, G, H are collinear. ∎

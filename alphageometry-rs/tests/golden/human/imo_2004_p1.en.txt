Let F be the foot of the perpendicular from B to AR.

Claim 1. F, N, B, A are concyclic.
Proof. ∡BFA = 90° = ∡BNA (FB ⟂ AR; BC is a diameter), so F, N, B, A are concyclic.
Claim 2. FB = NF.
Proof. ∡FAB = ∡NAF (AR bisects ∠BAC). By equal arcs and chords (Claim 1), FB = NF.
Claim 3. R, N, F, O are concyclic.
Proof. ∡RFN = ∡BFN + 90° = 2∡FBN + 90° = 2∡RAN + 90° = ∡BAC + 90° = ∡MCN (FB ⟂ AR; FB = FN (Claim 2); ABNF cyclic (Claim 1); AR bisects ∠BAC; BC is a diameter). As drawn, ∠MCN = ½·∠MON = ∠RON (central angle ∠MON = 2∠MCN; OR bisects ∠MON), so R, N, F, O are concyclic.
Claim 4. R, A, F, P are collinear.
Proof. AB·AM = AC·AN (power of A with respect to circle (BMCN)). By the radical axis theorem (P, R, M, B are concyclic; P, R, N, C are concyclic; B, A, M are collinear; C, A, N are collinear), R, A, F, P are collinear.
∡CAB = ∡NCM + 90° = ∡NBA + 90° = ∡NFA + 90° (BC is a diameter; BCMN cyclic; Claim 1). ∡PCB = ∡NBO + ∡(CP, OF) + 90° = ∡(CP, AB) + ∡ROF + 90° = ∡PCN + ∡NRA = ∡PNC + ∡CRA = ∡CRA + ∡PCO₂ + 90° = ∡PRA = 0° (OF is the perpendicular bisector of BN (Claim 2); OR bisects ∠MON, OB = OM, OB = ON; Claim 3, ∡CAB = ∡NFA + 90°; CNRP cyclic; O₂C = O₂N, central angle ∠CO₂N = 2∠CPN; O₂C = O₂R, central angle ∠CO₂R = 2∠CPR; Claim 4), so P, B, C are collinear. ∎

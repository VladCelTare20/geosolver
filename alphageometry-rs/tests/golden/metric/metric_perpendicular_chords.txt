EUCLIDEAN PROOF
  Goal:  dist(A,C)^2 + dist(B,D)^2 = 144

  1. Let A' be the point diametrically opposite A on the circle (so AA' is a diameter through the centre O).
  2. |OA| = 6 (given), so OA² = 36.
  3. O is the midpoint of AA', so AO² = ¼·AA'².  [from 1]
  4. AA' is a diameter of the circle, so ∠ABA' = 90° (Thales' theorem — an angle in a semicircle is right).  [from 1]
  5. AA' is a diameter of the circle, so ∠ACA' = 90° (Thales' theorem — an angle in a semicircle is right).  [from 1]
  6. CD ⟂ AB and BA' ⟂ AB, so CD ∥ BA'  (two lines perpendicular to the same line are parallel).  [from 4]
  7. In right triangle ACA' (right angle at C), by the Pythagorean theorem AA'² = CA² + CA'².  [from 5]
  8. CD ∥ BA' are parallel chords of the circle, so they cut equal arcs and CA' = BD (equal chords): CA'² = BD².  [from 6]

  Combining the equations above gives dist(A,C)^2 + dist(B,D)^2 = 144  (= 144). ∎

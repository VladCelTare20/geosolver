EUCLIDEAN PROOF
  Goal:  dist(A, B) ^ 2 + dist(A, C) ^ 2 = dist(B, C) ^ 2

  1. In right triangle BAC (right angle at A), by the Pythagorean theorem BC² = AB² + AC² (the right angle at A is given).

  Combining the equations above gives dist(A, B) ^ 2 + dist(A, C) ^ 2 = dist(B, C) ^ 2  (= 0.2882). ∎

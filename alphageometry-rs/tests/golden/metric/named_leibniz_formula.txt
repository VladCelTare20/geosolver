EUCLIDEAN PROOF
  Goal:  dist(P, A) ^ 2 + dist(P, B) ^ 2 + dist(P, C) ^ 2 = dist(G, A) ^ 2 + dist(G, B) ^ 2 + dist(G, C) ^ 2 + 3 * dist(P, G) ^ 2

  1. |_5A| = |_5C| (given), so _5A² = _5C².
  2. _4 is the midpoint of BC, so B_4² = ¼·BC².
  3. _5 is the midpoint of AC, so A_5² = ¼·AC².
  4. By Apollonius's median theorem in triangle ABC with median A_4: AB² + AC² = 2·A_4² + 2·B_4².
  5. By Apollonius's median theorem in triangle _5BC with median _5_4: _5B² + _5C² = 2·_5_4² + 2·B_4².
  6. By Apollonius's median theorem in triangle PBC with median P_4: PB² + PC² = 2·P_4² + 2·B_4².
  7. By Apollonius's median theorem in triangle BAC with median B_5: BA² + BC² = 2·B_5² + 2·A_5².
  8. By Apollonius's median theorem in triangle GAC with median G_5: GA² + GC² = 2·G_5² + 2·A_5².
  9. Facts derived from the hypotheses by the deductive closure:
       ↳ 001. assumption: coll B C _4
       ↳ 002. collinear: B _4 C [001]
       ↳ 003. assumption: cong _4 B _4 C
       ↳ 004. assumption: coll A C _5
       ↳ 005. collinear: A _5 C [004]
       ↳ 006. assumption: cong _5 A _5 C
       ↳ 007. assumption: coll A _4 G
       ↳ 008. collinear: A G _4 [007]
       ↳ 009. assumption: coll B _5 G
       ↳ 010. collinear: B G _5 [009]
       ↳ 011. segment arithmetic: |B_4| ↔ |C_4| (add/mul transfer) [003]
       ↳ 012. segment arithmetic: |A_5| ↔ |C_5| (add/mul transfer) [006]
       ↳ 013. segment arithmetic: |AC| ↔ |A_5| (add/mul transfer) [005 & 006 & 012]
       ↳ 014. segment arithmetic: |BC| ↔ |B_4| (add/mul transfer) [002 & 003 & 011]
       ↳ 015. similar triangles: △CBA ∼ △C_4_5 [002 & 005 & 013 & 014]
       ↳ 016. similar triangles: △ABG ∼ △_4_5G [002 & 008 & 010 & 014 & 015]
  10. G lies on A_4 between A and _4, and |AG| : |G_4| = 2 : 1 (derived from the hypotheses).  [from 9]
  11. AG:G_4 = 2:1. By Stewart's theorem for the cevian BG in triangle BA_4: BG² = 1/3·BA² + 2/3·B_4² − 2/9·A_4².  [from 10]
  12. AG:G_4 = 2:1. By Stewart's theorem for the cevian _5G in triangle _5A_4: _5G² = 1/3·_5A² + 2/3·_5_4² − 2/9·A_4².  [from 10]
  13. AG:G_4 = 2:1. By Stewart's theorem for the cevian PG in triangle PA_4: PG² = 1/3·PA² + 2/3·P_4² − 2/9·A_4².  [from 10]

  Combining the equations above gives dist(P, A) ^ 2 + dist(P, B) ^ 2 + dist(P, C) ^ 2 = dist(G, A) ^ 2 + dist(G, B) ^ 2 + dist(G, C) ^ 2 + 3 * dist(P, G) ^ 2  (= 2.5767). ∎
